Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains how Vellora Living Automation handles information while supporting the creation and publishing of editorial content for the Vellora Living Instagram account.

1. Information We Process

The service may process the Instagram account identifier, access credentials required for publishing, generated images and captions, publishing status, and technical logs needed to operate and troubleshoot the service.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate the content workflow, prepare and publish content to the authorized Instagram account, monitor publishing results, and diagnose technical problems.

3. Storage and Security

Access credentials should be stored in protected environment secrets and must not be published in source code or public repositories. Reasonable measures are used to limit access to credentials and operational data. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

4. Sharing and Sale of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared with Meta and Instagram as necessary to authenticate the account and publish content. Hosting or automation providers may also process information needed to operate the service.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Operational logs and generated content may be retained for as long as needed to operate, maintain, and troubleshoot the service. To request deletion of information associated with this automation, contact us using the email below.

6. Third-Party Services

Instagram and Meta process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Use of this service is also subject to the applicable policies of the hosting and automation providers.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The latest version will be published on this page.
