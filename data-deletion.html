Data Deletion Instructions

This page explains how to request deletion of information associated with Vellora Living Automation, which supports content creation and publishing for the Vellora Living Instagram account.

1. Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of information associated with this service, email us using the address below. Use the subject line Data Deletion Request and explain that you want your information deleted.

Email: suliman.7surkhrody@yahoo.com

You do not need to include your password, access token, or other secret credentials in your request.

2. What to Expect

We will review your request and, where applicable, delete information associated with the service that is under our control, including stored account identifiers, publishing credentials, generated drafts, and operational logs.

We may contact you if we need reasonable information to identify the relevant records. We will handle eligible requests as soon as reasonably possible, subject to applicable legal obligations.

3. Revoke Instagram Access

You can also revoke the app's access through Instagram's settings. Open Instagram, go to Settings, then Apps and Websites, find the connected Vellora Living automation app, and remove its access if that option is available.

Revoking access helps prevent future access through that authorization. It does not automatically delete information already stored by the service.

4. Content Already Published

Content already published to Instagram may remain on Instagram until it is removed there. To remove a published post, use Instagram to delete that post separately.
